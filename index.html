02 — Selected work

What I built this year

Three projects from first year. Each one lists the tools, what I was responsible for, and what I would do differently next time.

Astrododge

Unreal Engine 5 · Solo · 2 months

A 3D sci-fi in which you have been sent to the OISS (Orbital Irish Space Station) to rescue its crew. You must rescue the stranded crew mates while using your personal thrusters to navigate through the station, dodging debris and asteroids along the way. 

My role: Everything — Blueprint classes, spawning, scoring, HUD and the win/lose game states.

Super Santa

Unreal Engine 5 · Solo · 3 weeks

A 2D christmas themed platformer. The evil Krampus has kidnapped poor Mrs. Claus! Take control of St. Nick and rescue Mother Christmas from Krampus’ clutches, defeating his nutcracker minions along the way!

My role: Everything — Blueprint classes, spawning, scoring, HUD and the win/lose game states.

Draugr

Unreal Engine 5 · 2 Person Team · 5 months

A 3rd person action, adventure game for PC that is inspired by Norse mythology. The player will take on the role of a recently deceased man that must complete tests for the chance to return to life and not become a “Draugr” an undead evil creature.

My role: Music, sound effects, level design.

03 — About

About me

I'm a second-year Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh campus. I came to the course with a strong passion for video games, how they work and what makes them great. Now I want to make my own.

I am most interested in gameplay programming — the systems that decide how something feels to control. This year I have worked in C++ and Blueprints, modelled and textured my own assets, and recorded and implemented my own audio.

Next I want to go deeper into C++ and take on a longer team project.

Strengths
